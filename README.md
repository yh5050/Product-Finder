# Deskside: tech unboxing marketplace prototype

A clickable, high-fidelity prototype of a two-sided marketplace where short-form creators find paid opportunities to unbox or demo desk and tech accessories, and small brands find creators whose past videos fit their product.

**Live demo:** see the GitHub Pages link in this repo's About section.

## Demo flow

1. **Brand view → Campaign builder.** Review the keyboard brief (live offer preview on the right) and click **Publish brief**.
2. **Creator view.** The keyboard appears in Maya's matched feed. Open it, check **Offer terms**, pick a past video, review, and **Apply**.
3. **Brand view → Applicants.** Maya ranks first on relevant portfolio work. **Shortlist** her.
4. **Creator view.** The application now shows **Shortlisted**.

Use **Reset demo** in the top bar to start over.

## Notes

- All brands, creators, metrics and terms are fictional sample data.
- Product images are illustrative stand-ins for real photography.
- Payments, shipment tracking and social account connections are out of scope.
- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step: open `index.html` or serve the folder.
